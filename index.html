three threads of research

#DD5144

individual differences in color perception

people differ in how they see and think about colors. but are those differences random, even within groups of similar people? I use triadic similarity judgments to create personal color spaces for people, then test whether those differences predict behavior on interference and categorization-generalization tasks.

dissertation
#A42D70

How visualization design shapes interpretation

every visual feature used in a data visualization affects how we interpret it. details like colors or outlines can result in completely different assumptions about what a visualization is communicating. how does changing these visual features affect people's interpretations of data? how does that affect their decisions, especially about hazard maps?

design × cognition
